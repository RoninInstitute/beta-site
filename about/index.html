    About Us

    California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation (Incorporated 2025)

    Our Vision

    To foster a global community of scholars pursuing truths and sharing knowledge for the common good within a culture of belonging.

    Our Mission

    The Ronin Institute for Independent Scholarship 2.0 (RIIS 2.0) advances the pursuit and dissemination of knowledge across disciplines by providing independent scholars with scholarly affiliation, community, opportunities for collaboration, and support for the dissemination of their work.

    We provide an academic community that transcends traditional institutional boundaries for scholars committed to truth, empathy, and the pursuit of knowledge in the public interest. RIIS 2.0 seeks to enable scholars to share ideas, develop their capabilities, establish collaborations, and pursue scholarship in ways compatible with their individual circumstances.

    Our Values and Principles

    Truth and Empathy are RIIS 2.0's founding values. Belonging is a foundational principle that shapes how those values are expressed in our community, governance, and operations.These values guide what we do and how we do it:

    Truth

    • Scholarship seeks to deepen understanding through disciplined inquiry, evidence, reasoning, interpretation, and open exchange appropriate to each field.
    • We pursue scholarship in good faith, remaining open to new evidence, ideas, interpretations, and the possibility that our conclusions may change.
    • We also recognize that “truth” may look different in each field, and we welcome many scholarly approaches.

    Empathy

    • We pursue our scholarship with the intention of benefiting society.
    • We consider the broader impacts of our research and its effects on others, and we approach our work with empathy, kindness, and care.
    • We uphold high ethical standards in research and take care to avoid potential harms that could arise from our research.
    • We seek to consider the effects of our scholarship on others and to pursue our work responsibly and with regard for potential benefits and harms.

    Belonging

    • We are, most importantly, a community. We approach each other and those around us with kindness and care.
    • Every scholar is seen, heard, and valued as part of a supportive global community.
    • We cultivate psychological safety, dignity, and mutual respect.
    • We are committed to enabling an environment of peer-to-peer support.
    • We are invested in each other’s successes and understand that the creation of knowledge is not a zero-sum game.
    • A multiplicity of perspectives, disciplines, and experiences strengthens our shared pursuit of knowledge.
    • Belonging means creating a space where all can contribute freely and authentically.

    Our Goals

    • Advance scholarship in the public interest. Provide affiliation, community, and opportunities for dissemination that help independent scholars contribute knowledge and perspectives to their fields and to society.
    • Broaden participation in scholarship. Create pathways for people with a wide range of backgrounds, circumstances, and experiences to pursue meaningful scholarly work outside conventional academic career structures. Transformative insights occur when scholars approach their subject from new perspectives, which is often derived from life experiences.
    • Support humane and sustainable scholarly lives. Foster an environment in which scholars can pursue research at an intensity and in a manner compatible with their lives, responsibilities, resources, and individual goals.
    • Build a scholarly community. Help scholars find one another, exchange ideas and experiences, develop intellectually, and form collaborations and self-organized communities around shared interests.
    • RIIS 2.0 does not itself conduct, sponsor, direct, or endorse the individual research of its affiliated scholars.

    Our Governance

    RIIS 2.0 is governed by a Council elected by Fellows. In addition, Working Groups advise the Council and help run key aspects of RIIS 2.0 as part of a do-ocracy model, but ultimate responsibility resides with the Council. The current Interim Council, consisting of six Fellows is governing until the first full elections can be held.
