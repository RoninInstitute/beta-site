    About Us

    California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation (Incorporated 2025)

    Our Vision

    A global community of scholars pursuing truth and sharing knowledge for the common good within a culture of belonging.

    Our Mission

    The Ronin Institute for Independent Scholarship 2.0 (RIIS 2.0) advances the pursuit of knowledge across all disciplines by supporting independent scholars through ethical governance, institutional credibility, and collaborative community. RIIS 2.0 enables rigorous, innovative, and socially responsible research outside traditional academic structures to contribute meaningfully to the public good and the progress of human understanding. Our mission is to provide an academic community for people who are committed to truth and empathy, that transcends traditional academic boundaries.

    Our Values

    These values guide what we do and how we do it:

    Truth

    • The main purpose of scholarship is to discover and communicate things that are true.
    • We believe in the importance of pursuing our scholarship in good faith. That means being open to new ideas, and to the possibility that we might be wrong.
    • We also recognize that “truth” looks different in each field, and we welcome many scholarly approaches.

    Empathy

    • We are, most importantly, a community. We approach each other and those around us with kindness and care.
    • We are committed to enabling an environment of peer-to-peer support.
    • We are invested in each other’s successes and understand that the creation of knowledge is not a zero-sum game. We pursue our scholarship with the intention of benefiting while avoiding harm.

    Belonging

    • Every scholar is seen, heard, and valued as part of a supportive global community.
    • We cultivate psychological safety, dignity, and mutual respect.
    • A multiplicity of perspectives, disciplines, and experiences strengthens our shared pursuit of knowledge.
    • Belonging means creating a space where all can contribute freely and authentically.

    Our Goals

    • Broaden the pool of scholars, creating avenues for more people to do scholarship
    • Create new avenues for people with a broader set of experiences to contribute to scholarship by doing the work they feel most compelled to do. Transformative insights occur when scholars approach their subject from a new perspective, which is often derived from life experiences.
    • Foster a humane approach to scholars and scholarship.
    • Create a healthy and supportive environment where people and their scholarship can thrive, allowing people to pursue research in ways that work for them, as part of a fulfilling life.
    • Create a community to help scholars find each other, share experiences, and cooperate. Scholars need a community of people with whom they can share ideas and grow intellectually. We aim to help scholars establish new contacts and collaborations, self-organizing in ways that support their work best.

    Our Governance

    RIIS 2.0 is governed by a Council elected by Fellows. In addition, Working Groups advise the Council and help run key aspects of RIIS 2.0 as part of a do-ocracy model, but ultimate responsibility resides with the Council. The current Interim Council, consisting of six Fellows is governing until the first full elections can be held.
